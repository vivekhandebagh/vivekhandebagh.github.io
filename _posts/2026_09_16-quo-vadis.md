There’s a scene in the apocryphal Acts of Peter. After the crucifixion of Christ, Peter has found his way out of Rome in escape from the persecution. He finds himself at the crossroads of Via Appia, when his teacher appears in front of him.

> “Domine, quo vadis?”
> Lord, where are you going?

> “Romam eo iterum crucifigi.”
> I am going to Rome to be crucified again.
