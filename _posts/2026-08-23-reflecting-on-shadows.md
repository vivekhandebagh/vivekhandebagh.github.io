> A camera is a device for learning how to see without a camera.
> —Dorothea Lang

This quote struck me with enough energy for me to begin taking intentional photos more regularly. I now try taking at least one photo a day, and I try messing with only one parameter at a time. The goal for me here is not to necessarily to become a professional photographer (although I welcome it, if it so happens), but to train my aesthetic intelligence. I've touched on why I feel this is important in "Beauty as Saving Grace."
