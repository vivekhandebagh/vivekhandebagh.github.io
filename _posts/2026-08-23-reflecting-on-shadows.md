> A camera is a device for learning how to see without a camera.
> —Dorothea Lang

This quote struck me with enough momentum for me to begin taking intentional photos more regularly. I now try taking at least one photo a day, and I try messing with only one parameter at a time. The goal for me here is not to necessarily to become a professional photographer, but to train my aesthetic intelligence. I've touched on why I feel this is important in [Beauty as Saving Grace]({% post_url 2026-01-25-beauty-as-saving-grace %}).

Today was my first morning in [Keshavam](https://www.instagram.com/keshavamdc/) and I was completely enchanted. I must've spent at least an hour and half staring and taking photos. Coming from the west coast, I'm not as accustomed to the Victorian ornamentation.
